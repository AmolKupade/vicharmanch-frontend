Trust & Safety

Community
Guidelines.

VicharManch is a place for authentic voices, respectful debates, and creative freedom. Help us keep this space safe and inspiring for everyone.

Our Golden Rule

"Treat others' ideas with curiosity, and their presence with respect. We are building a library of human experiences, not a battlefield for egos."

🤝

Respect & Kindness

Attack the idea, not the person.

Debate is encouraged; toxicity is not. We do not tolerate harassment, bullying, doxxing, or hate speech of any kind. You are free to disagree with a post, but you must do so respectfully in the comments.

✓ Do this

Provide constructive feedback, share alternative perspectives, and report abusive behavior.

✕ Don't do this

Use slurs, insult a creator's intelligence, or organize downvote brigades.

✍️

Original Content Only

Your voice is what makes this platform special. Plagiarism ruins the experience for everyone.

📚

No Copy-Pasting

Do not publish content that you did not write. If you are quoting another source, use the Blockquote tool and clearly link to the original author.

🤖

AI-Generated Content

While you can use AI tools to outline or brainstorm, publishing 100% raw AI-generated articles without disclosure is frowned upon and may be downranked by our algorithm.

🚫

No Spam Policy

Self-Promotion

Do not use articles solely to drop back-links to your commercial products or courses without providing real value first.

Clickbait

Titles must accurately reflect the content. Misleading titles designed just to farm clicks will be flagged.

Keyword Stuffing

Write for humans, not for SEO bots. Articles stuffed with hidden or repeated keywords will be removed.

Enforcement & Actions

Zero Tolerance Violations

If a user is found violating our core safety guidelines (e.g., severe harassment, illegal content, or malicious hacking attempts), we will take immediate action.

  • 1 Content Removal without prior notice.
  • 2 Temporary suspension of publishing rights.
  • 3 Permanent account ban and IP block.
