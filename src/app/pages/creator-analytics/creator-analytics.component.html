Editorial Rules & Best Practices

Cover Image Ratio

Always use high-resolution images. Our platform automatically crops cover images to a 16:9 ratio for the feed, and 2:1 ratio for the article header. Avoid putting text at the very edges of your cover image.

16:9
Safe Zone for Text

Accessibility (a11y)

VicharManch is for everyone. Always add Alt Text (Alternative Text) to your images. This helps visually impaired readers using screen readers understand your visual content, and it also heavily boosts your Google SEO.
