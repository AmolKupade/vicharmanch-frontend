Partner Program
Guidelines.

Before you apply to monetize your content on VicharManch, please ensure your profile and articles align with our strict quality and safety standards.

On this page

1. Eligibility Criteria

To maintain a high standard of discourse, the VicharManch Partner Program is not open to everyone by default. You must meet the following baseline requirements before submitting an application:

  • ✓ Minimum Content: You must have published at least 5 high-quality, original articles on your profile.
  • ✓ Account Age: Your account must be active for at least 30 days.
  • ✓ Clean Record: You must have zero strikes or community guideline violations in the past 6 months.
💡

Pro Tip

Engagement matters more than follower count. We look for creators who generate thoughtful discussions in their comment sections.

2. Quality Standards

We do not monetize "diary entries" or low-effort posts. Partner content is distributed to thousands of readers, so it must provide immense value.

Originality & Depth

Your articles should offer a unique perspective. Rewriting news articles or summarizing Wikipedia pages will result in your application being rejected. Dive deep, share personal experiences, and back your claims with data.

Formatting & UX

A giant wall of text is unreadable. Use H2 and H3 tags, bullet points, and high-quality cover images. Content that is visually poorly formatted will not be monetized.

3. Prohibited Content

Even if you are accepted into the program, publishing any of the following will result in an immediate suspension of monetization privileges:

Strictly Forbidden

  • Undisclosed 100% AI-generated articles.
  • Plagiarism or copyright infringement (DMCA violations).
  • Clickbait titles that do not deliver on their promise.
  • Get-rich-quick schemes, unregulated crypto advice, or MLMs.
  • Hate speech, harassment, or graphic violence.

4. Monetization Rules

Once accepted, you will start earning based on the reading time and engagement your articles receive from premium users and ad-shares.

Sponsorships & Affiliates

We allow creators to include external affiliate links or sponsored mentions, provided they follow the FTC disclosure guidelines. You must explicitly state if a post contains affiliate links at the top of the article.

Limit: Maximum of 3 affiliate links per article.

5. Review Process

What happens after you click submit?

1

Automated Screen

Our system checks your account for the baseline eligibility criteria (Account age, minimum posts, strikes).

2

Editorial Review

A human editor reviews your latest 3 articles to ensure they meet our depth and formatting standards.

3

Final Verdict

You will receive an email within 48-72 hours. If accepted, your monetization dashboard unlocks immediately. If rejected, you can re-apply after 30 days.

Ready to take the next step?

If your content meets all the guidelines above, we would love to have you in the Partner Program.
