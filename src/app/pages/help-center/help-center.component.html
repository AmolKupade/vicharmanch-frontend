Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my custom blog URL?

You can change your handle in the Profile Settings. Note that changing your handle will break any existing links to your previously published articles.

When do I get paid for my monetized articles?

Payments are processed on the 1st of every month for the previous month's earnings, provided you have crossed the $50 minimum threshold.

I accidentally deleted my draft. Can I recover it?

Yes! Our editor auto-saves versions. Go to your drafts folder, click on the 'three dots' menu, and select 'Version History' to restore an older save.
